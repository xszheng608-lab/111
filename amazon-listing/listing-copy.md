## Product description

Unimate is Unicity’s ultra-concentrated yerba mate drink mix in natural lemon flavor. It is a dietary supplement. The front of the box shows 3 g of yerba mate per serving and 0 g of added sugar per serving. Each box contains 30 single-serve stick packs and weighs 5.4 oz (153 g).

Empty one stick pack into a cup and stir it into hot or cold water. For this net weight, a common amount is about 12 fl oz (375 mL). Use more or less water to taste, and follow the directions on your package. Suggested use is one stick pack a day.

This product is a dietary supplement and is not a meal replacement. Yerba mate contains naturally occurring caffeine. It is not recommended for pregnant or nursing women, children, or people who are sensitive to caffeine. Do not exceed the suggested use.
